## Frequently asked questions

### Does Synapse send my recordings or notes to the internet?
No. Speech transcription and AI structuring happen **entirely on your device**, and Synapse collects no data. The only connection the app makes is the one-time download of its speech models; nothing of yours is sent in it. After that you can use it in Airplane Mode.

### What is the download it asks for on first launch?
Synapse turns your voice into text with Parakeet, an open speech recognition model, plus a small model that tells speakers apart. They take about 800 MB in total and download once from Hugging Face, with your permission. Wi-Fi is recommended. If you are coming from version 1.0, the app asks for this download again after updating and removes the previous model to free up space.

### Which devices are supported?
Synapse requires a device that supports Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with Apple Intelligence), with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings, under Apple Intelligence & Siri. Titles, summaries and action items come from Apple Intelligence on the device itself.

### Do I need to create an account?
No. Synapse has no sign-up, no email and no login.

### How do I record a note?
Open the app, tap the capture button, allow microphone access the first time and talk. You see the text as you speak. When you tap Stop, Synapse structures the note automatically. You can leave the app or lock the screen while recording.

### Does it tell who is speaking in a meeting?
Yes. If several people speak in the recording, the note shows each person's turns. Tap a speaker's name to rename it; the change reaches the title, the summary and the action items too.

### Which languages does it work in?
Transcription understands 25 European languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese. You can let it detect the language or choose it yourself.

### Can I convert an audio file I already have?
Yes. In WhatsApp, Telegram, Voice Memos or Files, share the audio and choose Synapse. On iPad you can also drag one or several audio files onto the app window.

### Why does a note stay in the queue?
If you leave the app while a note is being processed, or the device is busy, the note waits and finishes by itself when you come back. The recording is not lost.

### Does it work on Apple Watch and CarPlay?
Yes. You can capture ideas from your Apple Watch without taking out your phone, and dictate notes in CarPlay. The note is structured on the iPhone.

### Where are my notes stored?
On your device, in the app's local storage. If you delete the app, the notes are deleted. We recommend exporting the ones you want to keep.

### How do I delete my data?
All your data lives only on your device. To remove it completely, uninstall the app.
